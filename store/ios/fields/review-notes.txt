BullDozer: World in Numbers is a native Flutter app. Every chart, the flat world
choropleth and the spinning globe, the values scatter, the bubble player, country
profiles, data stories and the quiz are drawn natively — none of them is a web
page. External links (a dataset's source, an article, the Edu reads) open in an
in-app Safari view rather than leaving the app; that is the only browser surface
and it always shows a page from a public site, never app content.

There is no account, no login, no in-app purchase, no advertising and no
user-generated content. No demo credentials are needed: every screen is reachable
from a cold launch.

Data comes from a public JSON feed at azenha.ai/bulldozer, built from open
sources — IMF, World Bank, Gapminder, WVS, V-Dem and others. Each dataset is
credited on screen with its licence and unit. Country summaries and images come
from Wikipedia under CC BY-SA and are attributed in place.

ASK AI (BETA)
The Ask AI tab writes a short answer about a country from our published
figures. To see it: open the Ask AI tab, type "Brazil" and pick it. Before the
first question leaves the device, a dialog explains that the question and the
chosen country are sent to our server (bot.azenha.ai) and on to Claude by
Anthropic, with a Cloudflare Workers AI model as the fallback. "Not now" sends
nothing and the figures stay on screen; "Allow" continues and is remembered.
Any number in an answer that is not in our data is rejected on the server, and
every answer carries a note that AI can be wrong.

PRIVACY
There is no analytics or crash SDK, no advertising identifier and no login.
Release reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device. Network
traffic is anonymous HTTPS GETs for the public feed and Wikipedia summaries,
plus the Ask AI question after the user allows it. That question is not linked
to the user, carries no identifier, is not stored by us and is not used for
tracking; the App Privacy label declares it as Other User Content, used for App
Functionality.
