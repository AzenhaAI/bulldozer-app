BullDozer: World in Numbers is a native Flutter app. Every chart, the flat world
choropleth and the spinning globe, the values scatter, the bubble player, country
profiles, data stories and the quiz are drawn natively — none of them is a web
page. External links (a dataset's source, an article, the Edu reads) open in an
in-app Safari view rather than leaving the app; that is the only browser surface
and it always shows a page from a public site, never app content.

There is no account, no login, no in-app purchase, no advertising and no
user-generated content. No demo credentials are needed: every screen is reachable
from a cold launch.

Data comes from a public JSON feed at azenha.ai/bulldozer, built from open
sources — IMF, World Bank, Gapminder, WVS, V-Dem and others. Each dataset is
credited on screen with its licence and unit. Country summaries and images come
from Wikipedia under CC BY-SA and are attributed in place.

ASK AI (BETA)
The Ask AI tab writes a short answer about a country from our published
figures. To see it: open the Ask AI tab, type "Brazil" and pick it. Before the
first question leaves the device, a dialog explains that the question and the
chosen country are sent to our server (bot.azenha.ai) and on to Claude by
Anthropic, with a Cloudflare Workers AI model as the fallback. "Not now" sends
nothing and the figures stay on screen; "Allow" continues and is remembered.
Any number in an answer that is not in our data is rejected on the server, and
every answer carries a note that AI can be wrong.

LINKS FROM THE WEBSITE (NEW IN 1.40)
Indicator pages on our website open in the app when it is installed (Universal
Links for azenha.ai/bulldozer/dataset/* and /bulldozer/economy/). To see it,
open https://azenha.ai/bulldozer/dataset/imf-govt-debt in Notes or Messages on
a device with the app. Without the app the same link opens the web page.

PRIVACY
There is no third-party analytics or crash SDK, no advertising identifier and
no login. New in 1.40: the app counts anonymous daily totals (which screens are
used and in what order, features, which indicators and countries are opened,
time in app, error type and location, device language, phone or tablet, dark
or light mode, large text, and the install source on the first run) with its own small
module and sends them to our server (usage.azenha.ai, stored in the EU). There
is no device, account or advertising identifier, nothing the user types is
included, and the IP address is not kept. It can be switched off in the menu:
"Share anonymous usage statistics". The App Privacy label declares Product
Interaction, Other Usage Data and Crash Data, not linked to the user and not
used for tracking.
Release reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device. The Ask AI
question is sent only after the user allows it; it is not linked to the user,
carries no identifier, is not stored by us and is not used for tracking; the
label declares it as Other User Content, used for App Functionality.
The Edu tab's "App analytics" card links to an article about analytics tools;
the app itself contains none of them.
