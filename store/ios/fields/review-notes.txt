BullDozer: World in Numbers is a native Flutter app. Every chart, the flat world
choropleth and the spinning globe, the values scatter, the bubble player, country
profiles, data stories and the quiz are drawn natively — none of them is a web
page. External links (a dataset's source, an article, the Edu reads) open in an
in-app Safari view rather than leaving the app; that is the only browser surface
and it always shows a page from a public site, never app content.

There is no account, no login, no in-app purchase, no advertising and no
user-generated content. No demo credentials are needed: every screen is reachable
from a cold launch.

Data comes from a public JSON feed at shpara.com/bulldozer, built from open
sources — IMF, World Bank, Gapminder, WVS, V-Dem and others. Each dataset is
credited on screen with its licence and unit. Country summaries and images come
from Wikipedia under CC BY-SA and are attributed in place.

The app collects nothing. There is no analytics or crash SDK, no advertising
identifier and no login. Release reminders are local notifications scheduled on
the device; the only network traffic is anonymous HTTPS GETs for that public feed
and for Wikipedia summaries and images.
