Checked by a statistician, an economist, a data designer and a data journalist — and rebuilt where they found problems:

• Economy this week (Biz): GDP, inflation, jobs, bond yields and currencies for 43 economies, plus oil, gold and commodity prices — each figure with its own period and source.
• Median and mean on every ranking, and a median mark in every bar, so one country reads against the rest.
• Fairer ranks: equal values share a place, colours follow whether higher or lower is better, and forecasts are never ranked as facts.
• Uncertainty shown where sources publish it: WHO estimates come with their interval, and survey rankings show the range of places a country could hold.
• Trend lines only for steady trends, now corrected for year-to-year runs.
• Years in one scrolling row, opening on the latest actual year; a "Latest" view for surveys run in different years.
• Maps coloured in five classes with the values in the legend; the bubble chart has labelled axes and taps.
• Search forgives typos and suggests where to start.
• Statistics basics (Edu): median, percentage points, margins of error, trends — each with its formula and an example.
• Every chart and shared image carries the BullDozer Stats name and where to get the app.
• Fresh data: IMF October 2026 outlook, homicide rates from the UN (UNODC), Corruption Perceptions Index 2025.
