## Full description (EN, ≤4000 chars)
BullDozer turns the world into numbers you can actually explore.

• Charts — 150+ indicators across economy, society, business and the
  environment. Search, filter by topic, and rank every country with a period
  switcher; tap any country bar to see its trend over time.
• Countries — a searchable list with flags, then a full profile: a map zoomed
  to the country, headline macro KPIs, a short encyclopedic summary, and every
  indicator grouped into Statistics and Surveys. Compare up to 5 countries on
  one indicator side by side.
• Explore — plot any indicator against another across all countries to see how
  they relate (a bivariate scatter view).
• Maps — every dataset has a world choropleth coloured by the data; tap a
  country for its trend line.
• News — featured data stories with live mini charts and quick facts.
• Quiz — "Guess the country": ten rounds, data facts as hints, fewer hints for
  a higher score.

Fast, offline-friendly, light and dark themes. No account, no ads, no tracking.
Data comes from the public BullDozer feed at shpara.com/bulldozer.
